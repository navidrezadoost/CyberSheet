## Boundaries retained

- Owner-backed `WorkbookSheetView` reads and retained raw Worksheet reads are distinct contracts. A structural `workbookId` property check does not establish authoritative ownership.
- Internal adoption tests demonstrate behavior for a deliberately constructed fixture; they do not establish a supported public Workbook path that publishes the raw handle.
- The async retained-raw Paste characterization stops at the command-manager boundary. The synchronous retained-raw Paste test exercises real command execution and observes rejection at `clearDependencies()` for its one-cell case; it does not establish general rollback atomicity.
- Retained-raw Cut confirms internal payload staging and an attempted browser write. The mocked browser write rejects, so successful external clipboard publication is not established.
- Contract approval, production implementation authorization, exact-key disposition promotion, R1 acceptance, and release/merge authorization are separate decisions.

Review and approve, reject, or revise each proposed policy independently. Until that decision and separate implementation authorization are recorded, these are recommendations only. No implementation or ledger disposition change follows from this memorandum.
