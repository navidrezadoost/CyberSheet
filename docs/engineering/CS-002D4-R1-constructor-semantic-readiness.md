## Interpretation

- FindService and ExportPlugin have confirmed cached-value read findings for specific internal retained-raw lifecycles. Those findings leave the owner-transition contract undecided and do not establish a supported first-party caller-supplied Worksheet adoption route.
- DataManager has confirmed zero-based failures only for the tested sort/filter/group configurations. This does not establish behavior for every configuration or the raw-after-adoption lifecycle.
- AccessibilityManager construction fails on the initial zero-based address with a real Worksheet and cleans up before publication. Formula-to-ARIA disclosure remains unverified because successful construction has not been established.
- EditingManager runtime evidence covers copy, cut, document paste, numeric/date/non-date-string/other-value fill, and all three specifically exercised failed-undo paths (fill, cut, paste). Standalone paste undo restores value without restoring style; paste undo does not demonstrate full reversal of the standalone paste operation. Manager-listener disposal remains unresolved.
- All semantic implementation dispositions remain `open-not-reconciled`; this artifact promotes none. R1 acceptance remains withheld.
