## Findings kept distinct

- FindService, EditingManager, and ExportPlugin have specific formula-cache disclosures through internally retained raw Worksheets. None establishes a supported first-party raw Worksheet adoption route.
- DataManager's row-zero failures establish standalone compatibility defects for the tested configurations; they do not establish formula disclosure or safety after adoption.
- AccessibilityManager's constructor failure currently prevents its suspected formula-to-ARIA path from being reached with a real Worksheet.
- EditingManager's standalone paste-undo style loss, failed fill/cut/paste undo history loss after adoption, and persistent document listeners are separate findings. None is a Worksheet mutation bypass.
- The constructor-service ownership contract remains provisional. “Verified behavior” in the readiness table records evidence, not acceptance of an ownership policy.

## Exact-key and governance boundary

The [55-key readiness inventory](CS-002D4-R1-constructor-semantic-readiness.md) supplies the frozen semantic keys and current evidence annotations for all five services. The matrix above identifies the per-service evidence needed to support a later disposition review; it does not inherit dispositions across records. All 55 implementation dispositions remain open. No production or test changes, frozen-register edits, or ledger changes are authorized by this synthesis. R1 acceptance remains withheld.
