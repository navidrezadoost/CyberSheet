## Readiness dimensions

- **Technical evidence readiness** means evidence exists to assess current behavior or a bounded gap. It does not mean the desired behavior is approved.
- **Contract decision readiness** means the question is sufficiently bounded for an authorized owner to decide. Owner designation and channel are still prerequisites for formal decisions.
- **Implementation authorization** is a separate, explicit permission to change production behavior after the contract is decided.
- **Integration test reachability** describes whether supported public routes can exercise the behavior. A compile-blocked or unsupported fixture is not integration evidence.
- **Release acceptance** is the overall R1 gate; none of the evidence below independently satisfies it.
